# Landshop editorial media provenance and reuse limits

**Updated October 31, 2026.** This register separates photographs sourced under the Pexels License, owner-provided reference photographs, and illustrative imagery. The public site uses photographic references of modern low-rise duplexes in place of the previously rejected urban high-rises. The official property listings must always use approved original photographs of the specific estate or unit.

Owner-provided photo references may have third-party copyright or model/property restrictions; this repository records their source **but does not establish commercial publication rights**. The operator must confirm those rights separately before relying on them in a permanent paid campaign. None of the references may be used as a verified property listing photo or attributed to a specific Landshop estate without evidence.

## License and release verification

- Pexels: https://www.pexels.com/legal-pages/license/
- Pexels permits commercial use but prohibits implying a depicted person or brand has endorsed the advertised service. Individual recognizable-model and private-property rights can still need permission for specific commercial uses.
- Editorial photography is not a substitute for commissioned Landshop talent, site photos, or video. For a permanent brand hero featuring identifiable Nigerian people, obtain the photographer's and models' written usage approvals where needed.
- Do not misrepresent edited, computer-generated or third-party-owned images as actual property photography.
- Do not repurpose photos as logo assets or resell them.

## Production image quality standards

- Use responsive real photos, keep natural skin texture and genuine architecture.
- Hero crop should preserve facial features and building geometry, leaving headline space.
- Avoid sexualized styling, obviously synthetic people, duplicated faces, watermarking and exaggerated HDR.
- Use sizes approximating 1600–2000 px for large hero shots; page cards should load lazily.
- Maintain descriptive alt text that clearly distinguishes editorial photos from real Landshop property assets.
- For approved listings, the property owner/developer must upload their original unaltered media; never borrow a third-party listing photo.

## Scope of replacement and media safety

- Replaced non-listing imagery on the homepage carousel, three product cards, three product landing pages, About, Contact, Leadership/team categories, the seven-form directory, mortgage detail pages, Thrift, Services, and property education/Insights pages with sourced Lagos photography.
